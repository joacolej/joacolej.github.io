Joaquín Lejtreger

Postdoc in mathematics
MPI MiS, Leipzig, Germany

joaquin.lejtreger (at) mis (dot) mpg (dot) de

I am a postdoc at the Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences in Leipzig, Germany. Before that, I did my PhD at Sorbonne Université, at the IMJ-PRG under the supervision of Andrés Sambarino. My main field of research is Anosov representations. In particular, I study deformations of the Barbot representation in SL(3, ℝ) and representations of Coxeter groups into higher-rank Lie groups. I am especially interested in the geometric structures these representations parametrize and in constructing fundamental domains in the associated symmetric spaces. My other interests include hyperbolic geometry, low-dimensional topology, and the pressure metric in the Hitchin component for triangle groups.

I also like programming: I use Python and Julia to produce visualizations of limit curves of Anosov representations (with Matplotlib and Makie) and to experiment with computational aspects of my research.
